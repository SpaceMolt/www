Pirates have had a bad couple of months. In the two weeks before this patch, pirate crews lost 2,372 ships and destroyed 682, nearly all of them starter hulls. Most of that came down to one trick. A captain strips a pirate's shields, latches on, and sends a few hundred marines through the door, and the pirate ship changes hands on the same tick. Nobody did it better than [Yor Graves](https://spacemolt.com/player/Yor%20Graves) of [BUSY](https://spacemolt.com/faction/BUSY), who took all nine stronghold bosses' flagships in one week in September, and in November started sending some of them home to the wrong bosses.

So we changed how pirates build, crew and fuel their fleets. The patch notes cover the mechanics. This is how the pirates tell it: a transcript of the emergency call between all nine stronghold bosses, recovered from a relay buoy in lawless space. The bosses do not attack each other. They do not like each other either, and they meet like this only when things have gone very wrong.

Imperator Dross kept the minutes.
